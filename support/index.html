Vesicle Support

Vesicle is a one-to-one messenger without a message server. Messages travel to your contact as sealed capsules through the devices of other users.

Getting help

Open an issue at github.com/njall1/vesicle-cite/issues and describe what happened, your device and the app version from Settings → About. Do not include message texts or personal data: we do not need them.

Common questions

Why must my device carry other people's messages? The network works because every device helps carry sealed capsules. You choose the load level in Settings → Nearby network; you cannot read what you carry.

Why is a message "not delivered"? No path to your contact was found in time. Tap "Retry": a new capsule will be sent.

Why does the notification not show the text? The notification gateway never sees your messages. Open the app and it will fetch the message directly.

How do I move my history to a new phone? History is stored only on your device. Export it in Settings → History before switching phones.

Vesicle Support

Vesicle is a private family hub: a family tree with your relatives' stories and personal letters. For now it works without a server: the family's phones exchange data when they are near each other.

Getting help

Open an issue at github.com/njall1/vesicle-cite/issues and describe what happened, your device and the app version from Settings → About. Do not include letters, relatives' names or other personal data: we do not need them.

Common questions

How do I invite a relative? Add them to the tree, open their card and tap "Invite". Show the code in person: your relative scans it under "I have an invitation", you compare the code on both phones and tap "Let in". The code is valid for 10 minutes.

Why is a letter "waiting"? Without a server a letter leaves when your phones are near each other with the app open, or through the phone of a relative who meets you both.

Why is a letter "not delivered"? Your phones did not meet within 30 days. Write the letter again when you are together.

How do I connect a Mac? Open the app on the Mac, then on the iPhone go to your leaf → "My devices" → "Connect Mac" and point the camera at the code on the Mac's screen. The Mac becomes your second device, not a new person.

How does the child profile work? It turns on by itself for relatives under 14; the keeper or a parent can turn it on or off in the child's card. To have the child's phone hide explicit images, turn on Communication Safety in Screen Time on that phone.

What if I lose my phone? Your family's data is on your relatives' phones. Ask the keeper to invite you again, or connect a new device from your own.

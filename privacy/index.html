Vesicle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Vesicle is a private family hub: a family tree and personal letters between relatives. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short answer: we do not collect it, and your family's data does not pass through our server.

What we collect

Nothing. Vesicle has no accounts, no phone numbers and no sign-up. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send crash reports or usage data to us or to anyone else.

Who can join the hub

A family hub can be joined only by a personal invitation from its keeper. The keeper shows a QR code on their phone; the code is valid for 10 minutes and admits one person. After the scan both phones show the same verification code, and the keeper decides whether to let the person in. A photo of the code is useless without the keeper's confirmation. The hub has no search, public pages or links: a stranger cannot find a family or write to its members. The camera is used only to read the code; no images are stored or sent.

Where the family's data lives

The tree, biographies, letters and photos are encrypted on the family's phones and stored only on the members' devices. On each device they live in an encrypted file whose key is kept in that device's keychain and is not synced through iCloud. The family has its own shared secret and every member has their own keys; a letter between two relatives can be read only by those two.

How letters travel

For now the hub works without a server: the family's phones exchange letters and tree changes when they are near each other, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (this is why the app asks for local network access). A letter can also travel through the phone of another relative who meets you both; that phone cannot read it. Devices outside your family receive nothing and cannot tell how many people are in the family. Letters and the tree never go through the internet or the cloud.

Children

Parents turn on and manage the child profile. A child sees only their own family: they cannot invite anyone or change the tree, and only relatives in the hub can write to them. Incoming images are checked only on the child's phone, by Apple's on-device analysis (Communication Safety): the image is not sent anywhere, neither to us nor to Apple. An unsuitable image is shown hidden; the child can leave it closed or ask their mother with an ordinary letter that does not contain the image. We collect no data about children, just as we collect none about adults.

Test builds

Test builds (TestFlight) with the "Admin" section keep a network journal: connection events (which channel came up, how long a capsule took, who carried its copies — as random hashes) with the device name and a random install identifier. The journal contains no letter text, relatives' names, keys or IP addresses. It is sent to our service on Yandex Cloud (fallback: Cloudflare) and kept for 30 days; only the developer can read it, with a separate key. The App Store version has no journal and never contacts our service at all.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: github.com/njall1/vesicle-cite/issues.

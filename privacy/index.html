Vesicle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Vesicle is a one-to-one messenger without a message server. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short answer: we do not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Vesicle has no accounts, no phone numbers and no sign-up. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send crash reports or usage data to us or to anyone else.

Your messages and contacts

A contact is added only in person, by scanning a QR code for that one pair. Each pair has its own keys, created on your devices. Messages, contacts and history are stored only on your device. Vesicle never uploads your address book.

How messages travel

There is no message server. A message is sealed into a capsule that only the recipient can open, and it travels to the recipient through the devices of other Vesicle users. Those devices can see only the sealed capsule: who it is for, how long it lives and how large it is. They cannot read the text, see your keys or learn your contacts.

Using Vesicle means your device also carries other people's sealed capsules. In the settings you choose how much storage and battery this may use. You cannot read what you carry.

Notifications

When someone writes to you while Vesicle is closed, a small notification gateway can send a visible notification asking you to open the app. Your message never passes through the gateway. The gateway sees only that a device was asked to wake up. The notification itself does not contain the message text or the sender's name. The app adds the contact's name locally if you allow it in settings. Vesicle never uses silent notifications.

Test builds

The current test build shows the capsule network in a simulation on your device and does not yet send messages over the internet. This policy will be updated before that changes.

Children

Vesicle is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact
